## Getting started

### What it does & how to get there

- **Search & claim (locate an existing Marketplace application and take it over)**
  - *Path:* Home page → "Search Marketplace" feature. Article states: "Agents can use search & claim to locate the consumer's existing application and continue working with it inside the HealthSherpa account."
  - This is the mechanism for pulling a consumer record that was started elsewhere (another agent, healthcare.gov direct, another platform) into your own HealthSherpa account. The Getting started article NAMES the feature but gives zero step-by-step. Full procedure is in the Dashboard collection — 'Dashboard overview' (https://faq.healthsherpa.com/en/articles/11404356-dashboard-overview). This is the single most AOR-relevant capability named in the whole collection.
- **Import existing book of business**
  - *Path:* Dedicated article: 'Importing clients/leads' — https://faq.healthsherpa.com/en/articles/2789488-importing-clients-leads (linked from 'New to HealthSherpa? Here are the basics')
  - Article text: "Agents can import an existing book of business or use search & claim to locate individual consumer records." No file format, column spec, or row limit is given in the Getting started collection — those details are in the linked article.
- **Quote button (shop/quote without a full application)**
  - *Path:* Home page → "Quote button"
  - Initiates plan shopping and quoting before completing an application. Works in ALL states including non-supported SBMs — quoting is universal, submission is not. Deeper article: 'Plan shopping' https://faq.healthsherpa.com/en/articles/2789795-plan-shopping
- **Start application (subsidy application)**
  - *Path:* Home page → "Start application"
  - Begins the subsidy/APTC application. Deeper article: 'Applying for a subsidy' https://faq.healthsherpa.com/en/articles/5315741-applying-for-a-subsidy
- **Carrier settings — set licensed states and carriers (controls submission NPN)**
  - *Path:* Settings → "Carriers" tab
  - CRITICAL for commissions: 'Creating an account' states enrollment submissions for a HealthSherpa for Agents account are "processed via agent's carrier settings or agency override NPN." This setting is what determines which NPN lands on the enrollment — i.e. who becomes agent of record and who gets paid.
- **ACA checklist (post-signup setup checklist)**
  - *Path:* Home page → "ACA checklist"
  - Three items named explicitly: set licensed states/carriers, establish or join an agency, evaluate Enrollee Assistance Program (EAP) eligibility.
- **FFM account integration (enables EDE pathways)**
  - *Path:* During signup: Step 4 choose "Guided setup" (vs "Skip") → Step 5 select "Integrate FFM account" button → opens CMS portal in a new window → log in with EUA/IDM User ID using FFM credentials → Step 6 complete CMS identity verification for Enhanced Direct Enrollment (EDE)

### Gotchas

- SUBMISSION IS ONLY SUPPORTED IN FFM STATES + GEORGIA. Exact wording: HealthSherpa supports enrollment submissions for "All states that use the Federally Facilitated Marketplace (FFM)" plus Georgia's SBM, "Georgia Access." For every other SBM state: "agents can get a quote and shop for plans using HealthSherpa. When it's time to apply and complete an enrollment submission, HealthSherpa will direct the agent to the state's SBM website." This is the documented root of Ethan's Illinois / Get Covered Illinois blind spot — those clients structurally cannot appear in HealthSherpa data.
- THE GLOSSARY HAS NO AOR, NPN, COMMISSION, BOOK OF BUSINESS, FFM, SBM, CIC, EFFECTIVE DATE, OR TERMINATION ENTRY. The 'ACA glossary' article covers only consumer-facing ACA concepts (APTC, CSR, FPL, metal levels, deductible, coinsurance, OOP max, tax household, OEP, SEP, QLE, DMI, SVI, COBRA, STM, indemnity, ancillary, on/off-exchange, EDE, CMS, Marketplace Eligibility Notice). Do not expect this collection to define agent-of-record or commission mechanics — it does not touch them anywhere.
- Marketplace certification is required EVERY YEAR: "Marketplace certification is required each year to sell ACA plans." FFM and supported SBM platforms run through the CMS Marketplace Learning Management System (MLMS). Lapsing this before Nov 1, 2026 would take the entire book offline.
- CMS DOES NOT ALLOW ONE AGENT TO HAVE MULTIPLE FFM USER IDs, and FFM credentials cannot be shared. Each agent needs one unique FFM User ID. This caps any 'run a second account for the funnel' idea at the HealthSherpa layer only — the FFM layer is one-per-person.
- HealthSherpa accounts cannot be shared, and you cannot be logged into multiple accounts simultaneously. The +modifier email trick creates multiple accounts but not concurrent sessions.
- Agents certified for a supported SBM (Georgia is the named example) but NOT FFM certified must SKIP "Guided setup" and then "reach out to Agent Support to manually complete this step." There is no self-service path for that configuration.
- NIPR license verification is mandatory and automatic. At signup Step 3 you enter your NPN and check an authorization checkbox; CMS requires verification through the National Insurance Producer Registry. EAP accounts must separately "Allow HealthSherpa to conduct NIPR checks." A lapsed state license will surface here.
- Support hours are narrow and there is NO documented Open Enrollment extension. Phone Mon-Fri 6:00am-4:00pm PST; email and chat Mon-Fri only 6:00am-2:30pm PST. Closed on federal holidays. In Mountain Time chat/email die at 3:30pm MT. Plan OEP escalations around this — the article explicitly contains no extended-hours provision.
- Support tickets need FFM application IDs and screenshots WITH URLs, plus your NPN. Sending a client name alone will bounce the ticket.
- Enrollee Assistance (full-service) enrollments submit under HealthSherpa's NPN — you are NOT the agent of record and you are paid a per-enrollment fee, not carrier commission. Do not route existing book clients through EAP.
- Off-exchange medical and dental have "Limited carrier and state availability" — not a general-purpose fallback.
- Carrier appointments are required to sell on a carrier's behalf and "Requirements vary by state, carrier, and line of business." Being licensed is not sufficient.
- You cannot release yourself from an agency. Settings → "Agency affiliation" only gives you the administrator's contact info; the administrator must approve the release.
- The OEP 2027 live webinar is English only, 1 hour. No CE credit or certification is offered for any HealthSherpa webinar — none is mentioned anywhere on the training page.
- Article 'New to ACA? HealthSherpa is here to help' is largely orientation/licensing fluff for brand-new agents (get licensed, get LOA, get certified, get appointed). Nothing operational for an established 1,194-client book. Skip it.
- The 'New to HealthSherpa? Here are the basics' article NAMES search & claim and book-of-business import but gives no procedure for either — it is a hub page. The actual steps require opening the linked Dashboard-collection articles.

## Important steps

### What it does & how to get there

- **FFM account integration (first-time setup)**
  - *Path:* Gear icon (top right of dashboard) → "Account settings" → "Agent Profile" tab → "Federally Facilitated Marketplace (FFM) integration" section → "Integrate account" button. A new CMS portal window opens (https://portal.cms.gov/); choose auth method "EUA/IDM User ID"; enter FFM username (NOT case-sensitive) + password (case-sensitive). Success notice appears in the new tab — close the tab to return. Section then reads "Integrated". During account creation the equivalent entry point is the "Guided setup" button.
  - Links the HealthSherpa account to CMS credentials. This is the gate for everything EDE. Article states plainly: agents without an active FFM integration cannot search the Marketplace, submit applications, or view clients.
- **FFM re-integration after an inactivity timeout**
  - *Path:* Either the "Integrate account" button shown on the login screen, or Gear icon → "Account settings" → "Agent Profile" tab → FFM integration section → "Integrate account".
  - Integration lasts up to 12 hours IF the agent stays active. HealthSherpa auto-refreshes the session on mouse movement, typing, or scrolling. 30 minutes with none of those and you are prompted to reintegrate.
- **Remove a wrong FFM integration**
  - *Path:* Gear icon → "Account settings" → "Agent Profile" tab → FFM integration section → "Unintegrate FFM account" button.
  - The fix when the wrong CMS account got linked. Also the remedy path for the "FFM username mismatch" message (raised when the FFM username saved in HealthSherpa doesn't match the one entered at integration time).
- **Multi-factor authentication setup**
  - *Path:* Auto-prompted on first login after account creation. Otherwise: Settings page → "Two-factor authentication" section → "Edit authentication" button → pick "Text message" or "Authenticator app" → "Set up".
  - Text path: enter mobile number → "Send code" → enter the 6-digit code in the "Auth code" field → enter account password in "Current password" → "Enable two-factor auth" → recovery codes display → "Done". App path: install Google Authenticator, scan the QR code, then same Auth code / Current password / Enable two-factor auth / Done sequence. Both methods can be configured simultaneously on one account.
- **MFA recovery codes**
  - *Path:* Two-factor authentication page → "View codes" button.
  - Auto-generated at MFA setup, unique per account. The documented escape hatch when the phone is lost, broken, or dead. Article instructs saving them somewhere secure.
- **Enable EDE**
  - *Path:* "Enable EDE" button at the top of the HealthSherpa dashboard (or "Guided setup" during account creation) → integrate FFM account → complete identity verification form and submit → dashboard loads and the "Enable EDE" button disappears.
  - Agents not FFM-certified must contact Agent Support to have this step completed manually. The "Enable EDE" button can reappear later if FFM integration goes inactive or certifications lapse — its reappearance is a signal, not a glitch.
- **What EDE actually unlocks (full enumerated list from the article)**
  - *Path:* Available across the dashboard once EDE is enabled and FFM is integrated.
  - 1) Quote and shop plans; 2) complete and edit subsidy applications via accelerated workflow; 3) retrieve updated eligibility results; 4) submit enrollments and renewals; 5) identify and manage follow-ups in real time; 6) upload required documents; 7) view current application statuses; 8) make initial payments; 9) report Life Changes; 10) access and download Marketplace documents; 11) use reporting tools; 12) improve effectuation rates with post-enrollment tools.
- **Download Marketplace notices/documents for a client**
  - *Path:* Available through the EDE surface once FFM is integrated (blocked entirely when integration is inactive).
  - Exactly three document types are named: eligibility determination notices, 1095-A tax forms, and insufficient documentation notices. The third is the DMI/SVI paper trail.
- **Manage licensed states**
  - *Path:* Gear icon → "Account settings" → "Carriers" tab → "Add licensed states" section → pick state(s) from the dropdown → "Add" button. To remove: scroll to the state → "Delete" button.
  - HealthSherpa verifies licensure against NIPR at application time. Enrollment is hard-blocked if the agent is unlicensed in the consumer's state.
- **Set carrier appointments per state**
  - *Path:* Gear icon → "Account settings" → "Carriers" tab → scroll to the state → expand the "Edit" menu → check the box next to each carrier under the "You are appointed" column. Selections save automatically (no Save button).
  - Your NPN (or override NPN) is automatically applied to enrollments for any carrier that does NOT have EAP enabled. This is the setting that determines whose NPN lands on the enrollment.
- **Enrollee Assistance Program (EAP) carrier selection**
  - *Path:* Two routes. Route 1: Gear icon → "Account settings" → "Carriers" tab → state → "Edit" → check boxes under the "Enable for Enrollee Assistance" column. Route 2: Bonuses page → "Enrollee Assistance Program" tab → "Program settings". Autosaves.
  - When a carrier is EAP-enabled, enrollments submitted through EAP are processed under HealthSherpa's NPN instead of the agent's own NPN. The toggle is greyed out for carriers that don't participate in EAP.
- **Verify your own CMS/NPN standing when integration fails**
  - *Path:* Four public CMS datasets: AB Registration Completion List (https://data.healthcare.gov/ab-registration-completion-list), AB Suspension and Termination List (https://data.healthcare.gov/ab-suspension-and-termination-list), Agent Broker Registration Tracker (https://data.healthcare.gov/dataset/e4rr-zk4i), and Agent and Broker Marketplace Registration Status (https://data.healthcare.gov/ab-registration-tracker).
  - Use these to self-diagnose an integration failure: suspended/terminated NPN, incomplete training (accounts without completed training cannot be authenticated), or unsigned SHOP/HOD agreements. CMS credential recovery: username https://portaldev.cms.gov/wps/portal/unauthportal/selfservice/forgotuserid/, password https://portal.cms.gov/portal/forgotpassword.

### Gotchas

- THE 30-MINUTE FFM INACTIVITY RULE IS ALREADY LIVE. The CMS requirement took effect August 4, 2026 — roughly four weeks ago as of today. 30 minutes with no mouse movement, typing, or scrolling and the integration drops. HealthSherpa's 12-hour window only holds if you are actively driving the page.
- Blocked while FFM integration is inactive (explicit list): view clients in the dashboard; search for or claim a Marketplace application; submit, enroll, or finalize an EDE application; update personal information on an existing EDE application; cancel an effectuated policy; review or download Marketplace notices; submit follow-up documents.
- NOT blocked while integration is inactive: quoting and plan shopping; checking bonuses; working on SBM applications (Georgia is the example given); agency administration, NPN override, Shared book, Full book access; agency data exports — but only if the exporting account is itself FFM-integrated.
- EAP silently rewrites whose NPN is on the enrollment. Any carrier with "Enable for Enrollee Assistance" checked routes that enrollment under HealthSherpa's NPN, not yours. Nothing in the enrollment UI warns you at submit time.
- EAP is unavailable entirely in Florida, Iowa, Michigan, New Hampshire, North Carolina, South Dakota, and Wisconsin.
- HealthSherpa does NOT block an enrollment based on NIPR appointment status. It only blocks on state licensure. You can submit a policy with a carrier you are not appointed with, and the platform will let it through.
- NIPR data is not real-time. If appointment status looks wrong in the Carriers tab, HealthSherpa's instruction is to contact NIPR or the carrier — HealthSherpa will not fix it.
- Carriers use different names in different states, which is the article's stated reason agents "don't see" a carrier they are appointed with. Confirm the exact state-specific carrier name before assuming an appointment is missing.
- Double Redirect (DRD) was discontinued November 1, 2025 — agents can no longer redirect a consumer to HealthCare.gov as a fallback.
- The Marketplace Call Center no longer accepts agent-requested enrollments as of plan year 2027. Combined with the DRD shutdown, there is no backup path if EDE or FFM integration breaks mid-enrollment.
- MFA has been mandatory since August 13, 2025, per CMS. It is per-account — an agent with multiple HealthSherpa accounts must enable it separately on each one.
- Credential sharing is explicitly prohibited — including with assistants, IT, and agency staff. Only the account-owning licensed agent may log in and perform the FFM integration. CMS requires one unique FFM User ID per agent and prohibits multiple IDs per agent.
- Agency administrators cannot manage FFM integrations on behalf of their agents. There is no delegation path.
- Do not select "Keep me logged in" on a shared computer (article's explicit warning).
- The "FFM username mismatch" message fires when the username stored in HealthSherpa differs from the one typed at the CMS portal — you must pick the correct option on the screen to proceed.
- The Carriers tab does not exist for HealthSherpa Enrollee Assistance Full-Service accounts or carrier-specific account types. Enabling EDE is likewise not required for Full-Service accounts.
- Common integration failure causes named: missing characters/extra spaces/omitted punctuation in credentials, invalid or incomplete status in the CMS Enterprise Portal, incomplete required training, suspended or terminated NPN. Note the asymmetry — FFM username is not case-sensitive, the password typically is.

### Gotchas

- Export download links die fast: 'Export URLs expire approximately one hour after the export email is generated.' Any scheduled/automated pull must fetch the file inside that hour or the run is wasted. Exports History shows status but the link still expires.
- Exports arrive by EMAIL, not as an in-browser download. If the email doesn't show up, the only diagnostic is the Exports History tab status; a 'succeeded' export with no email means an email-settings problem.
- SSN, date of birth, and household income are ALWAYS masked with asterisks in exports (CMS restriction on EDE entities). Never build matching or dedupe logic that depends on DOB from a HealthSherpa export — it will silently be all asterisks.
- Filters set on the Clients or Leads page carry into the export. A filter left on from a previous session silently changes the CSV contents, and nothing in the file announces it. For automated pulls, reset filters explicitly every time.
- The agent-level Clients export has NO 'include archived' option — only date range, include-unsubmitted, and include-3-way-call-resolved-Invalid-Action. Archived rows can only be pulled in one shot by the AGENCY-level Enrollments export.
- Large exports are explicitly called out as slow: 'Large exports of thousands of clients can take additional time for the email to arrive.' At ~1,194 clients plus archived history he is in that zone.
- Archived records are hidden from the default view, so any count or filter run without touching the 'Archived status filter' silently excludes them. The filter options are All / Archived / Not archived.
- Agents cannot delete client or lead records at all. Archiving is the only mechanism for removing something from view — nothing is ever actually purged.
- Importing a CSV does NOT create an EDE connection. Imported records sit there with no live Marketplace sync until a sync is established through the enrollment process or search & claim. Imported clients can therefore look present but never update.
- Import templates are rigid: headers cannot be adjusted, columns cannot be rearranged, sample rows must be deleted, and the Clients template differs from the Leads template. A reordered column breaks the import.
- Consent uploads are one file at a time, under 20MB, and only .png, .jpg, .pdf, .mp3. No bulk upload of consent documentation.
- 'Enrollment review' consent (Eligibility Application Review) cannot be collected early — only 'Initial consent' (Consumer Consent) can be gathered before enrollment submission begins. Planning to front-load ALL consent before OEP will not work for the second type.
- Changing the Marketing page URL breaks every previously shared link — email signatures, ad creative, QR codes already in circulation all go dead.
- Marketing links can only enroll consumers in ON-exchange plans. Off-exchange remains agent-only, and enrollment is blocked entirely if the agent isn't licensed in the consumer's state.
- Changing the logo or color palette on the Marketing → Branding tab also changes the agent's own dashboard appearance — it isn't a consumer-only setting.
- Leads are plan-year specific and cannot be renewed. A lead from PY2026 does not roll into PY2027.
- The dashboard language toggle affects ONLY the agent dashboard. The consumer-facing branded enrollment site keeps its own language regardless.
- 'Providers & prescriptions' on a client record is visible post-enrollment only, and only to the writing agent.
- The Settings page in the left nav has been retired but still renders for some agents. Real settings live at Gear icon → Account settings. Editing the stale page is wasted effort.
- The Agency page (and every agency-level export, the Tasks tab, and the detailed Insights KPIs) is invisible unless the account is a primary admin or has been granted administrator access in an agency.
- Downline agents must have EDE enabled to be included in agency access management, NPN overrides, and several agency reports.
- Article warning on NPN Override: 'NPN overrides should not be used to avoid state appointment laws.'
- Agency admins can flip a 'Prevent exports' toggle that disables exports for direct downline agents — and that toggle does not cascade to sub-agency administrators.
- The Clients page, Leads page, Consent page, exports, imports, and archiving are all unavailable in HealthSherpa Enrollee Assistance Full-Service account types.
- The agent-level Insights page is essentially undocumented in this collection — one sentence, no metric list. Do not assume it contains the effectuation/renewal analytics described for the agency Insights tab.

## Consent

### What it does & how to get there

- **Two separate consents, and HealthSherpa renames them in the UI**
  - *Path:* Docs call them "Consumer Consent" and "Eligibility Application Review". The UI calls them "Initial consent" and "Enrollment review" (the Client Details tab / Consent Library columns) — and the Upload flow's dropdown calls the second one "Application review". Same thing, three names.
  - Consumer Consent = permission for the agent to assist. Required BEFORE collecting PII, searching the marketplace, shopping, applying, submitting, updating an application, checking coverage status, or refreshing an EDE data sync. Eligibility Application Review = the consumer confirmed the application info, plan selection and attestations. Required BEFORE submitting a new app, updating an app, submitting a plan selection, or completing an enrollment submission.
- **Consent page — the central hub**
  - *Path:* Dashboard > Consent. Two tabs: "Consent Library" (the default view when you open the page) and "Collect consent".
  - The Consent Library is the single place every consent lands, no matter where it was captured — enrollment flow, Client Details page, or the Consent page itself.
- **Consent Library — columns and statuses**
  - *Path:* Consent page > "Consent Library" tab.
  - Columns: Recipient (consumer name), Consent type ("Initial consent" / "Enrollment review"), Status, Updated at, Plan year, Channel (how it was captured), Action. Statuses: "Pending" (digital request sent, not completed), "Collected" (documented either in HealthSherpa or in the agent's own records), "Revoked" (agent marked it withdrawn).
- **Consent Library — search**
  - *Path:* Search box in the Consent Library. Searchable by status, consumer name, or email. Agency-level view also searches by plan year and shows the agency + agent tied to each consumer.
  - You can also "begin typing a consumer's name or email address to populate the list, or select the drop-down menu to scroll through all consumers." There are NO date-range filters, NO sort controls, NO multi-select, and NO export button documented on this page.
- **Consent Library — per-record Action menu**
  - *Path:* Consent Library > "Action" column on the row.
  - Four options: download the consent documentation, resend a consent request, mark consent as revoked, delete consent documentation.
- **Collect consent — digital request by email or text**
  - *Path:* Consent page > "Collect consent" tab > choose "Email or Text message" > enter the recipient's information > adjust the consent settings if desired > "Send consent request".
  - Consumer gets an email from no_reply@HealthSherpa.com, selects "Grant consent" (Consumer Consent) or "Review enrollment details" (Eligibility Application Review), enters the access code if required, reviews, electronically signs, confirms they agree to HealthSherpa's terms, and selects "Submit". A success message tells them the page is safe to close. A signed PDF is saved to the consumer's record automatically.
- **Collect consent — upload an existing document**
  - *Path:* Consent page > "Collect consent" tab > "Upload consent" > pick the consent type from the dropdown ("Initial consent" or "Application review") > choose file > "Upload". Also available from a client's record via Client Details tab > "Upload consent".
  - Accepts .png, .jpg, .pdf, .mp3 (so a recorded verbal consent is valid). Max 20MB per file. ONE file at a time.
- **Collect Consumer Consent EARLY — before any enrollment**
  - *Path:* Consent page > "Collect consent" tab. Works for Consumer Consent only.
  - This is the pre-OEP move. Eligibility Application Review cannot be collected early — it "must [happen] during enrollment submission because it must indicate application information and plan selection have been reviewed."
- **Capture inside the enrollment flow**
  - *Path:* Consumer Consent is collected on the "Privacy Policy" screen. Eligibility Application Review is collected on the "Confirm plan" screen.
  - On the Confirm plan screen you can leave the Eligibility Application Review dropdown minimized — doing so "assumes Eligibility Application Review will be documented and managed outside of HealthSherpa." That is a silent opt-out of the paper trail.
- **Access code for Eligibility Application Review**
  - *Path:* Generated by the system when you email/text an Eligibility Application Review request. The agent reads it to the consumer; the consumer verifies identity and enters it.
  - "The access code does not expire, but will not be visible after navigating past this page." To get a new one you must resend the digital consent form — which generates a new code.
- **Consent status in CSV exports**
  - *Path:* Standard Clients and Enrollments export reports (CSV, delivered by email).
  - Two consent data points appear: "Collected" = consent has been uploaded or marked as stored outside of HealthSherpa; "Action required" = the agent chose to upload consent later but has not yet done so. This is the only machine-readable consent signal HealthSherpa gives you.
- **Completion notifications**
  - *Path:* On by default; disable via Settings page.
  - Agent gets an email each time a consumer completes a digital consent form.
- **Downloadable blank consent forms**
  - *Path:* Linked at the bottom of the "Collecting & managing consent" article.
  - Standard Consent Form in English and Spanish, plus a separate Enrollee Assistance Program Consent Form.
- **Consent from the client record**
  - *Path:* Open a client > "Details" tab > consent section.
  - Shows the same record columns (type, status, updated, plan year, channel, action) scoped to that one consumer, and offers "Upload consent".

### Gotchas

- THERE IS NO BULK SEND. Nothing in any article documents multi-select, a select-all, a CSV of recipients, or a bulk consent campaign. The "Collect consent" form is single-recipient: one name, one email/phone, one "Send consent request". I checked this three ways (collection articles, the Consent page article, and a targeted search) and found no bulk path. For 1,194 clients this is the single biggest operational constraint.
- The access code vanishes. "The access code does not expire, but will not be visible after navigating past this page." If you navigate away mid-call before reading the code to the client, the only recovery is resending the form, which issues a NEW code — the old one you may have already texted them is now the wrong one.
- Eligibility Application Review CANNOT be collected in advance. It is locked to enrollment submission. Any pre-OEP consent drive can only bank the Consumer Consent half; you still have to capture the review half live on every single application during Nov 1 - Jan 15.
- Text consent silently disappears. "If a consumer has opted out of text messages from HealthSherpa, the text consent option will not be available." This is a HealthSherpa-specific opt-out, separate from his GoHighLevel/Agent Boost opt-outs — a client can be textable in GHL and untextable here.
- On the "Confirm plan" screen, leaving the Eligibility Application Review dropdown minimized silently assumes you are documenting it OUTSIDE HealthSherpa. It does not warn or block. Do that by accident and there is no record in the Consent Library, and the export shows nothing.
- Delete is permanent, and is restricted/blocked for Enrollee Assistance Program Shared-Service Model enrollments.
- File upload is one file at a time, 20MB max, and only .png/.jpg/.pdf/.mp3. No multi-file, no ZIP.
- CMS explicitly rejects weak documentation: "copy/pasted emails or texts into Word or PDF files" and typed signatures without evidence of consumer action are called out as inadequate. Self-rolled documentation is a compliance trap — the platform's signed PDF is the defensible artifact.
- 10-year retention is mandatory for every consent record. That is a data-retention obligation that outlives any client relationship and outlives his HealthSherpa subscription — worth mirroring into his own storage.
- CMS specifies no expiration for consent and no standard format. There is no built-in "consent expires, re-collect" nag. Nothing in HealthSherpa will tell him a 2024 consent is stale; he has to decide his own refresh cadence.
- The Consent Library has no date-range filter, no sort, and no export button of its own. Consent status only leaves the platform through the standard Clients/Enrollments CSV exports as "Collected" / "Action required".
- The Google-indexed collection URL /collections/7197439-consent is dead and redirects to Platform Updates. Bookmark /collections/19456531-consent.
- The consent-column definitions are NOT in the export article body — they live in external Box "export legend" documents for agent and agency exports. He'll need to open those to get exact column headers for parsing.

## Agency accounts

### What it does & how to get there

- **Create an agency (unlocks the Agency page)**
  - *Path:* Gear icon (top right) → "Account settings" → "Agency affiliation" tab → "Create a new agency" section → enter agency info → click "Create agency". An "Agency page" then appears in the dashboard nav.
  - The agent who creates it becomes "primary admin." Account-type rules: "HealthSherpa for Agents" accounts CAN create an agency and may only join a "HealthSherpa for Agents" upline. "Enrollee Assistance Full-Service" accounts CANNOT create an agency. Carrier-specific accounts can create one but may only join a same-carrier upline. Nothing in the article requires a minimum number of downline agents — creating a solo agency is what exposes the Agency page and its admin tabs.
- **Agency page — tab inventory**
  - *Path:* Dashboard → "Agency" page. Tabs across the top: Overview, Tasks, Insights, NPN override, Exports, Consent Library, Settings.
  - Visibility rule: "Only primary admins and agency admins will see the Agency page within their HealthSherpa dashboard." Overview is the default landing view and shows agent count + client count, downline agents, sub-agencies, and pending agent invitations from the past 30 days.
- **Application sync — bulk-refresh Marketplace/EDE data without opening each client**
  - *Path:* Agency page → "Tasks" tab → "Application sync". Adjust filters first, then hover over a consumer's row to trigger the sync request. Status changes to "Sync requested".
  - Explicit purpose per the article: request refreshed data sync between Marketplace applications and HealthSherpa for downline consumers, "eliminating the need to open applications individually." Syncs may take UP TO 1 HOUR. Once synced, the row moves to the bottom of the list (list is sorted by last sync date). Refreshed data then shows on the Clients page, in all in-platform reports, AND in exports. Requires consent. Applications where EDE access has been lost are EXCLUDED from this list.
- **Tasks tab — prebuilt filtered client worklists**
  - *Path:* Agency page → "Tasks" tab. Views available: Client follow-ups, Intake, Invalid Action, Renewals, Application Sync.
  - Can be filtered at parent-agency level with an option to include sub-agencies. "Invalid Action" and "Renewals" are agency-level versions of lists Ethan currently works one-by-one.
- **Insights tab — agency KPI reporting including effectuation**
  - *Path:* Agency page → "Insights" tab. Sections: agent metrics, submission analytics, Leaderboard, Submission segments, Effectuation metrics, Renewals, Sub-agencies.
  - "Effectuation metrics" is the notable one — effectuation is whether the consumer actually paid first premium, i.e. whether the policy is real and commissionable. "Renewals" and "Submission segments" are aggregate views. Parent-agency level by default with optional inclusion of sub-agency data.
- **Consent Library — central store of every consent record**
  - *Path:* Agency page → "Consent Library" tab. Search by plan year, status, consumer name, or email.
  - Shows associated agency and agent for each record. Actions available: view consent, download consent, revoke consent, and collect new consent. This is the only documented place to see consent status across a whole book at once rather than per-client.
- **Exports tab — agency-level exports**
  - *Path:* Agency page → "Exports" tab. For the payouts report specifically: Exports tab → "Export" button in the "Actions" column, then choose a date range.
  - Two functions: (1) generate exports at the agency level (broader scope than an individual agent's own export), and (2) DISABLE export functionality for direct downline agents. The Payouts program report is pulled here by date range.
- **Shared book — agent-to-agent client/lead access**
  - *Path:* Grant: Agency page → "Overview" tab → "Shared book" column → check the checkbox next to each direct downline agent. Use: Clients page or Leads page → "Clients filter" dropdown → toggle "Shared" vs "Mine".
  - Intended for call-center models, captive agents, and teams covering each other's consumers. A shared-book agent can service shared clients exactly like their own. When editing an application they can keep the existing NPN, change it to their own, or pick an agency override NPN. Notes created by shared-book agents are visible to all shared-book agents. HealthSherpa emails the agent when shared book access is granted or removed.
- **Full book — agency-wide client/lead access, explicitly pitched as AOR protection**
  - *Path:* Grant: Agency page → "Overview" tab → "Full book" column → check the checkbox next to each direct downline agent. Use: Clients page or Leads page → "Clients filter" dropdown → select "Agency" (vs "Mine").
  - Gives the selected agent access to the clients and leads of ALL direct downline agents plus their own. The article states it "helps prevent unwanted Agent of Record changes by preserving the last National Producer Number used" — i.e. when another agency agent touches the application, the last-used NPN is preserved instead of flipping. Notes are visible to all full-book agents. Email notification on grant/remove.
- **Administrator access — delegate admin rights**
  - *Path:* Agency page → "Overview" tab → "Admin" column → primary admin checks the box next to an agent.
  - An "agency admin" can: view the full agency hierarchy, grant/remove Shared Book and Full Book, access the Tasks and Insights tabs, manage NPN override settings, export agency-level reporting, manage agency export settings, use the Consent Library tab, update agency details and copy the join code, manage email settings for direct downline agents, and view carrier settings for direct downline agents. RESERVED to the primary admin only: granting/removing administrator access, and joining an upline agency.
- **NPN override — choose which NPN goes on a submission**
  - *Path:* Set up: Agency page → "NPN override" tab → "My override NPNs" section → "Add override NPNs" → pick NPN from dropdown → "save". Remove via "Delete" next to the record. Toggle: "Allow my direct downline agents to use their own NPNs" (Yes = default). Use at submission: the "Confirm your plan" page shows an NPN dropdown.
  - When an override NPN is selected, HealthSherpa applies that NPN "regardless of carrier settings." CRITICAL: "the consumer will appear in the writing agent's Clients page regardless of the NPN selected" — the Clients page does NOT tell you whose NPN was actually submitted. Setting the toggle to "No" forces agents to pick only from the override list.
- **Join code / invite agents**
  - *Path:* Find the code: Agency page → "Settings" tab. Agent joins via: Gear icon → "Account settings" → enter join code. Admin invites via: Agency page → "Invite agents" → enter email addresses in the popup → "Send email".
  - Join codes are unique per agency and are NOT case sensitive. Invited agents auto-link on account creation. Each licensed agent needs their own HealthSherpa account — accounts cannot be shared. Pending invitations show on the Overview tab for 30 days.
- **Join an upline agency / link a sub-agency**
  - *Path:* Agency page → "Settings" tab → "Upline affiliation" → enter the upline's join code → "Join". Downline agents check their own affiliation at: Settings page → "Agency affiliation" tab.
  - Only the PRIMARY ADMIN of an existing agency can link it as a sub-agency. A primary admin controls features for direct downline agents but NOT for sub-agencies or the sub-agency's own downline.
- **Email customization for downline agents**
  - *Path:* Agency page → "Settings" tab → "Emails" section. Customize client and agent email settings via dropdowns; enable agent emails via checkboxes. Signature: "View or edit email" → check "Use customized signature text".
  - For certain emails the admin can allow per-agent control, FORCE enabled for all agents, or FORCE disabled for all agents. Signature edits apply to all direct downline agents. Supported bracket fields that auto-populate: [Agent name], [Agency name], [Agency phone], [Account email]. When an admin forces an email on or off, a message appears on the Settings page of each direct downline agent's account.
- **Agency payouts — redirect bonuses to the agency**
  - *Path:* Request: Agency page → "Settings" tab → "Agency payout" → "Change payout structure". Tax setup: same path → "Set up W-9 payout details" → enter SSN or EIN. Resend contract: Agency payout → "Edit settings" → "Resend contract".
  - Redirects Enrollee Assistance Program, Medicare Referrals, Broker Bonuses, and Associates program earnings from individual agents to the agency, for internal redistribution. Flow: confirm role → HelloSign email to sign the agency joint marketing agreement (JMA) → HealthSherpa verifies and returns a countersigned contract → Bill.com emails a payment-method invitation after W-9 approval. Default payment is a paper check to the W-9 address; Bill.com ePayments enables bank transfer. Only the PRIMARY ADMIN can request this.
- **Removing an agent or sub-agency from a downline**
  - *Path:* No self-service button. The primary admin emails a written request to Support@HealthSherpa.com, copying the affected downline agent or sub-agency primary admin, including name, NPN, and email address, with a clear statement of the action requested.
  - Two outcomes: "Release" = account holder KEEPS access and consumer records, agency loses visibility. "Terminate" = account holder LOSES access, agency KEEPS visibility. A downline agent wanting out must go through their agency's primary admin; only a sub-agency's primary admin can request removal from its upline.
- **AOR at risk tab (the EDE-permission-loss report)**
  - *Path:* Clients page → "AOR at risk" tab. Sorted most-recent first. Look for status "Marketplace disconnected".
  - Two documented causes: (1) the agent imported a record without ever establishing EDE permission, or (2) another agent engaged with the enrollment on a DIFFERENT EDE platform — which signals an AOR change happening outside HealthSherpa. Recovery, in order: refresh icon on the Clients page → open the client Details page (often auto-refreshes) → "Sync" button on Details page → "Enable EDE" button on Details page → Search & claim the consumer's existing application. If all fail, call Agent Support at (888) 684-1373.
- **Last synced indicator**
  - *Path:* Clients page → the "Payments" column shows it as an "As of" indicator. Also on each Client Details page.
  - Reflects when the most recent EDE data sync happened between the Marketplace application and the HealthSherpa account. The article does NOT publish an automatic sync frequency or schedule. Manual refresh paths are the same five as the AOR-at-risk recovery list.
- **Onboarding checklist items that touch existing workflows**
  - *Path:* Enable EDE: Dashboard → "Enable EDE" button at top. Carrier settings: Settings page OR Bonuses page → "Carriers" tab. Book upload: Clients & Leads pages → "Import" button. Branded enrollment site: Marketing page → customize landing page → share unique enrollment link.
  - 14-step agency onboarding sequence: certification (CMS MLMS, annual, by state) → carrier appointments → account creation → link agents → FFM account integration → enable EDE (requires identity verification) → review bonus programs → bonus payout structure → carrier settings (licensed states, appointments, carrier availability) → explore Agency page → attend office hours → branded enrollment website → upload book of business → begin enrollments.

### Gotchas

- SHARED/FULL BOOK AGENTS CANNOT EXPORT THE SHARED DATA. Stated verbatim: "Shared book agents can export client & lead data about their own consumers, but cannot export information about shared book clients or leads." Same restriction on Full book. So book-sharing gives on-screen visibility only — bulk data still has to come from the admin-level Exports tab.
- Application sync takes UP TO 1 HOUR to complete. It is not instant, and there is no documented batch-select — you hover a row at a time.
- Application sync SKIPS the clients that most need it: "Excluded applications: those where EDE access has been lost." The clients showing as Marketplace disconnected on the AOR at risk tab are exactly the ones the bulk sync will not repair. Those still need the manual five-step recovery.
- The Application sync list "does not update in real time if two administrators are viewing it at the same time," so two admins can duplicate sync requests on the same application.
- NPN override is BLOCKED in seven states — Florida, Iowa, Michigan, New Hampshire, North Carolina, South Dakota, and Wisconsin — where the writing agent's own NPN must be used. The override options are removed from the "Confirm your plan" page in those states. Ethan has 185 Florida clients, his #3 state.
- NPN override does not work for Off-Exchange enrollments and does not work when the consumer came in through a marketing link.
- NPN override is also "not permitted by certain carriers," and both the writing agent AND the agent whose NPN is selected must be appointed with the carrier in most states.
- The Clients page LIES about NPN: "the consumer will appear in the writing agent's Clients page regardless of the NPN selected." Whoever shows on the Clients page is not evidence of whose NPN was actually submitted or who gets paid.
- The Overview tab client count is inflated: "The client count on the Overview tab may include consumers who have not yet completed an enrollment submission." Do not reconcile against it.
- EDE is a hard prerequisite for every admin tool. Downline agents must have Enhanced Direct Enrollment enabled before an admin can grant Shared book, Full book, Administrator access, or add them as an override NPN.
- Enrollee Assistance Full-Service account types are locked out of nearly everything: cannot create an agency, cannot receive Shared book, Full book, or Administrator access, and are exempt from certification/carrier appointments/FFM integration/EDE.
- Account-type compatibility is enforced on linking. A "HealthSherpa for Agents" account can only join a "HealthSherpa for Agents" upline; a carrier-specific account can only join a same-carrier upline.
- LEAVING AN AGENCY IS NOT SELF-SERVICE AND NOT UNILATERAL. Removal requires a written email request to Support@HealthSherpa.com from the PRIMARY ADMIN of the upline — a downline agent cannot remove themselves. And the "Terminate" option strips the agent's access while the agency KEEPS visibility into the records. Read this before ever entering an FMO's join code.
- Agency payouts carry a MANDATORY 45-day waiting period before payout eligibility is even determined, and payouts "may take 60+ days to process." Default payment is a paper check with NO tracking available.
- The W-9 for agency payouts is valid only for the calendar year and must be RESUBMITTED every January 1, or payouts presumably stop.
- Agency payouts are unavailable on carrier-specific account types, and an upline cannot set up payouts for a sub-agency.
- A primary admin's control stops at direct downline agents — it does NOT extend to sub-agencies or a sub-agency's downline. Sub-agency primary admins must grant access to their own agents themselves.
- An upline admin can FORCE client-facing and agent-facing emails on or off for every direct downline agent, and can overwrite their email signature. An upline can also DISABLE the Exports tab for downline agents entirely.
- Join codes are not case sensitive, which makes them easy to guess/mistype into the wrong agency.
- Only primary admins and agency admins can even SEE the Agency page. If Ethan grants admin to someone later, that person immediately gets hierarchy visibility, Insights, Consent Library, and agency exports.
